EloPlay

Chess, Go, table tennis, esports. See your win odds from two ratings, and what a tournament does to your number.
Expected score = 1 / (1 + 10^((opponent - you) / 400)); new rating = rating + K x (actual - expected). Expected score counts a draw as half a point, so it is not the same as the chance of winning outright. Federations use their own K-factors and rules for new players, so this is the textbook formula, not an official rating. Runs in your browser, nothing stored.
